Three sessions, not one exhausting checklist

1 · At a desk

60–90 minutes. Preflight, phone sync, songs/notes, Prepare → Load → Show. Use labeled rehearsal content. No translation spending is necessary.

2 · At the church

90–120 minutes. Real outputs, pastor tablet, short bilingual translation, audio choices and recovery. Reuse the prepared service from session 1.

3 · Full rehearsal

45–60 minutes or one sermon. Run the workflow without coaching, then review the archive. Only do this after the short critical cases pass.

These are scheduling estimates, excluding setup trouble and fixes. Full-length recording attachment is a separate follow-up once delivered. Stop when a serious failure would make later results misleading; continue unrelated sections.

What counts as ready for a supervised pilot?

Every case for the features you intend to use should pass, with no unresolved data-loss, privacy, wrong-screen or essential-control failure. Both language directions must get bilingual meaning approval before calling both supported in practice. A skipped feature must be explicitly excluded from the pilot. One rehearsal gives evidence for that setup; it does not prove every phone or venue.

  • Stop the affected part: missing/overwritten notes, private content visible publicly, the wrong screen being changed, translation reversing a statement, unresponsive Stop or continuing new speech generation after it is disabled.
  • Fix before relying on it: repeated reconnect failures, inability to reopen a prepared service, unusable translation delay or a workaround requiring an expert each week.
  • Record for later: cosmetic spacing, wording and small inconveniences that do not alter meaning or interrupt the service.

Freeze versions during a run. If we fix a problem, record the new build, rerun the failed case and the related workflow. Do not erase notes, reset an account, revoke every device or uninstall an app as a routine response to a failure.

Give me a useful failure report

Use the notes field for the case: device/build → exact steps → expected → actual → time. For translation, include the source video timestamp, direction, profile, notes/speech choices, what you heard and what appeared. For sync, identify both labeled test notes and whether each device was online. Screenshots or a short screen recording help. Keep API keys, sign-in links and unrelated private content out of the report.
